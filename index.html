About The Artist

Primary Instrument

Ibanez Prestiege Series Electric Guitar

Hailing from the serene mountain landscapes of Gangtok, Sikkim, Tashi Bhutia is a visionary guitarist and sound architect. By fusing delicate Himalayan folk timbres with intricate progressive rock rhythms and ambient guitar soundscapes, Tashi has crafted a sound that feels both ancient and futuristic.

With over a decade of live performance experience across South Asia and Europe, Tashi’s compositions rely on unconventional open tunings, percussive acoustic tapping, and expressive lead phrasing that echo the rugged tranquility of his homeland.
